We've relaunched the ABT Digital Paper website with a rebuilt architecture,
faster page loads, and a direct-to-sales inquiry channel.

## What's new

- **Every product series in one view.** Base paper, PE-laminated base, food-grade
  paper, and RC-coated photo paper — with real specs, weight ranges, and format
  options for each.
- **Direct sample requests.** Fill in the inquiry form and it lands straight in
  our overseas sales inbox. Reply within 1–2 business days.
- **Full factory tour.** Aerial, wet-end paper machine, coating and calendar
  lines, QC lab, and finished-roll warehouse — all documented on the
  [About](/about) page.
- **Live compliance status.** Every certification (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, FSC
  Chain-of-Custody, UKAS, IAF) is filed, current, and shown alongside our
  REACH, EUDR, PPWR, and food-contact declarations.

## What's next

We're finishing custom email routing so `overseas@abtpaper.com` sends from
our own domain, and we'll be adding product data sheets and safety data
sheets as on-request downloads.

For sample requests, technical questions, or OEM projects, use the
[contact form](/#contact) — or email us directly.
